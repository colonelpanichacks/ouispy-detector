Fleet key

                    
Checking...

                    

                        
                        
                        
                    

                    
Every node needs the same key. Give it to a node over USB or through its setup portal: scan the QR on your phone, then paste it into the portal (Mesh section, Fleet key). It never goes over the radio. Anyone with the key can read and command your fleet, so keep it like a password.
