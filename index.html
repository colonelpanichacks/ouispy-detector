The flasher writes the complete image (bootloader, partition table, boot selector and app), so even a blank board comes up. Mesh fleets: this release talks ESP-NOW at the long-range 250 kbps rate. Flash every node in a mesh with the same release.

The DetectorDual Domain Edition

  


    

      [ BLE ]
      
Bluetooth domain

      
Passive BLE scanning with six signature classes: OUI prefix, full MAC, BT SIG company ID, 16-bit service UUID, name substring, and composite filters. Catches Axon body cams and Meta/Ray-Ban glasses even with rotating MACs.

    


    

      [ WiFi ]
      
WiFi domain

      
Promiscuous management-frame scanning for wildcard probe requests and beacons. Time-sliced with BLE — one radio, both domains. Research & I-PRO body-worn camera signatures by DeFlock Joplin.

    


    

      [ DB ]
      
OUI Database

      
One-click vendor signatures in the web portal: RING, AXON, FLOCK SAFETY, DJI, PARROT, SKYDIO, META / RAY-BAN and I-PRO body cameras, plus pentest gear: FLIPPER ZERO, PWNAGOTCHI and the Hak5 WiFi PINEAPPLE.

    


    

      [ MQTT ]
      
Home Assistant

      
Publishes detections to any MQTT broker with auto-discovery — the Detector shows up as a device in Home Assistant, blips on detection, returns to idle after 10 seconds.

    


    

      [ MESH ]
      
Node mesh

      
Any number of Detectors link over ESP-NOW long range: no access point, no pairing. Remotes relay detections hop by hop to one home node, which publishes them to MQTT. The home node anchors the mesh channel and the remotes follow it. An optional fleet key encrypts the mesh and signs every command.

    


    

      [ FLEET ]
      
Remote control

      
From the home node: live roster, push the filter list to every node, mute or unmute, reboot, and read each remote's status over the air. Every node streams TLM telemetry on serial.

    


    

      [ MAP ]
      
OUI-SPY Mesh dashboard

      
A local web app for the home node (home-dashboard/ in the repo): live map, node trails, searchable detection history and fleet control, from your desk or your phone on your own network. Runs on your computer; nothing goes to the cloud.

    


    

      [ LOCK ]
      
Burn-in mode

      
Lock the configuration and boot straight into scanning for fixed deployments. Hold BOOT 1.5s to clear the lock and return to config mode.

    


  






  
Troubleshootingcommon snags

  


    

      
?

      
Nothing shows up in the port picker

      
Try a different USB-C cable (charge-only won't work), install CH340 or CP210x drivers if your OS doesn't auto-detect, or hold BOOT while plugging in to force download mode.

    


    

      
?

      
Flash finished but I hear nothing

      
The buzzer may be muted in saved settings. The board is fine — join the snoopuntothem Wi-Fi and confirm the portal loads at 192.168.4.1.

    


    

      
?

      
192.168.4.1 won't load on my phone

      
Android deprioritises networks with no internet and routes browser traffic over mobile data. Turn mobile data off, or accept the "stay connected" prompt, then reload.

    


    

      
?

      
Detector AP disappeared after locking config

      
That's intentional — locking is a one-way seal. Hold BOOT during power-on to clear the lock and restore the config AP. Reflashing does not unlock it unless you choose erase in the flasher; otherwise NVS survives a reflash.
